Bengal Accountability & Recovery Ready Reckoner: Integrated Edition (2011–2026)
First-of-its-kind longitudinal accountability atlas spanning 15 years of Bengal governance — mapping systemic failures, recovery gaps, and institutional drift through a structured, evidence-layered ready reckoner format.
Pioneers a dual-lens methodology that integrates ground-level political accountability data with macro civilizational recovery metrics, offering an unprecedented diagnostic template for sub-national democratic performance.
